Scores You Haven’t Had. Yet.

Every standard best-of-three tennis score is one of 196 combinations: two sets straight, or a split decided by a 10-point match tiebreak (always logged as 1-0 on tennisrecord.com, win or lose). Paste a tennisrecord.com match-history link — or just a player’s name — and this pulls every season on record and shows which of those 196 lines they’ve actually posted.

Player names must match tennisrecord.com exactly. This checks every season from the start year below through the present. If more than one player shares that name, paste the exact match-history URL for the right one — the link will keep pointing at that specific player.
